NYU Stern EMBA Course Outline

MCOM-GB 2105 Strategic Communication

Professor Brian Hanssen • Washington, DC Residency

Course Summary

Aligning messaging across company elements (values, business strategy), engagement (what it says, to whom, how it listens), and stakeholder perception, all within social context.

STEP Framework

Social Context, Tenets, Engagement, Perception anchored around a bounded Issue/Event.

AIM Framework

Audience (who), Intent (do/think/feel), Message (what's in it for them & you).

Minto's Pyramid

Lead with answer/main ask first, followed by 2-3 supporting points with evidence.

ABC Technique

Acknowledge out-of-scope questions, Bridge smoothly, Communicate core message.
